# Milestones And Acceptance

Date: 2026-06-30
Status: milestones_v0.1_adjusted_2026-06-30

## 0. Acceptance Principle

A milestone is not complete because a document exists. It is complete when the
evidence listed here exists and directly proves the requirement.

Requirement:

```text
Complete local landing master plan package exists and contains roadmap,
requirements, workstreams, backlog, milestones, boundaries, review/receipt plan,
and package receipt.
```

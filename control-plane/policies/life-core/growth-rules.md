# Life Core Policy Module: Growth Rules

Policy ID: `life-core.growth-rules.v0.1`
Status: `current_growth_policy`
Source anchor: `agents-os-life-core-v0.1.local-mirror.md` section 10
Local scope: Agent growth events, reflection, behavior policy candidates

## Purpose

Keep Agent growth tied to experience, reflection, behavior policy, and later
verification rather than raw accumulation.

## Growth Chain

1. Growth Event
2. DailyNote or specialized hot memory
3. MemoMaster or DailyNoteManager organization
4. AgentDream reflection
5. Metis or ThoughtCluster cognitive mapping
6. Behavior Policy candidate
7. Memory or Review Gate
8. Commit
9. Growth Verification

## Executable Rules

- Growth cannot auto-expand permissions.
- Growth cannot delete failures.
- Growth cannot fake community consensus.
- Dream insights cannot directly modify life core.
- One temporary feedback event cannot become permanent personality without
  review.

## Hard Denials

- Do not treat growth notes as formal memory by default.
- Do not use growth language to bypass Review or Jenn confirmation.

## Receipt Rule

Growth-related receipts must identify whether they record an event, a
candidate, a reviewed behavior policy, or a verified change.

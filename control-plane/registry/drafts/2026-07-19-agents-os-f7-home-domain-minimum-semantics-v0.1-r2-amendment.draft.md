## 3. Exact equality rule

For every ordinal, the validator must:

1. resolve the exact R1 case at that ordinal;
2. require the exact canonical `case_id`;
3. require the closed complete `raw_input` object;
4. derive the decision from that raw input without trusting the claim;
5. require exact equality with the R1 claimed decision;
6. require exact equality with the R2 catalog row;
7. recompute the R1 row fingerprint and R2 anchor fingerprint;
8. require the entire ordered catalog fingerprint and root fingerprint.

Replacing a required reject/defer case with another locally valid favorable
case is a semantic failure even if the graph is fully re-signed.
